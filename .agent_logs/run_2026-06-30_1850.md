What you discovered.
The `bcrypt` module raises a `ValueError` if the password exceeds 72 bytes. While Pydantic validated the character length (max_length=72), multi-byte characters like emojis or accented letters could still exceed the byte limit, causing unhandled 500 server errors and presenting a potential Denial-of-Service vulnerability on both `/register` and `/login` endpoints.

What you fixed/added.
Added a `@field_validator('password')` to the `UserCreate` and `LoginRequest` models in `backend/app/main.py` that explicitly encodes the password to UTF-8 and ensures its byte length is 72 or less. Added tests for this specific boundary condition.

Next Recommended Target
I suggest reviewing other Pydantic models in `backend/app/main.py`, such as `WorkspaceCreate`, for potential byte-length validation vulnerabilities in their text fields.
